# LankaAlert V2.4 — Feature Status

## Included in the preview
- Home overview with weather snapshot and 24/72-hour rainfall outlook
- Illustrated flood / landslide map with layer controls
- River-gauge illustration and refresh affordance
- Smart safety center with risk summary, preparedness score and location controls
- Alerts and forecast tabs with example notices
- Hazard report form with local-only report review queue
- Community updates, announcements and disaster archive
- Shelter browser, offline safety guide, emergency-call links, audio briefing, SOS confirmation and local safety check-in
- Eight-item readiness checklist saved only in this browser
- Search shortcut; language and district setup; profile, text scale, dark appearance, contrast, reduced motion and low-data controls
- User-triggered geolocation request and browser notification-permission control

## Not connected
- No weather, rainfall, river, map, hazard, community or government APIs
- No backend, cloud database, real authentication, OTP delivery or profile synchronization
- No message or emergency request is sent by SOS/check-in controls
- No photo upload; report images are previewed locally only

## Removed
- Road status page, its bottom-navigation item and related report examples.

## Safety note
The displayed values, alerts, river readings, shelter capacity and historical entries are illustrative preview data, not live official data. Do not use them for real emergency decisions.
